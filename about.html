Who We Are

A Little Bakery with a Big Heart

BakeBloom started with one small oven and one big dream: to make every celebration a little sweeter. What began in a home kitchen is now a loved bakery brand, trusted by thousands of families.

Every cake, cookie, pastry and chocolate is baked fresh each morning using quality ingredients and traditional recipes, with a modern touch.
